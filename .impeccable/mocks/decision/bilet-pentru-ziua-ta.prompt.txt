Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web app design direction comp, 1440x1024 landscape
Primary request: Design a complete first viewport for WeatherWise, a real weather plus activity and movie recommendation app. Direction name: "Bilet pentru ziua ta". This must be a polished usable interface, not a poster, scrapbook, or mood board.
Structure: a clean white navigation band with WeatherWise, location search for "Iași, România", saved locations, reminders and language. Beneath it, build the selected day as one large horizontal travel coupon: left field has destination, date, giant "22°" and "Mai mult senin"; middle field holds rain, wind, UV, sunrise and condition icons; right field holds one decisive action "Deschide itinerarul". A curved route line and one compact Iași destination photo may cross the coupon, but all data stays readable. Under the main coupon, render the next 7 forecast days as connected detachable stubs. Continue below with an open ticket wallet: morning, afternoon and evening itinerary on the left; weather-matched recommendations "Grădina Botanică", "Palatul Culturii", "Cinema în seara asta" on the right; include a precise but compact 14-day chart as printed route data. Show enough second-fold content to prove the language extends across the entire app.
Visual system: jet-age airline ticket wallet translated into a contemporary premium digital product. Crisp white coupon stock, carrier navy #164B72, official red #E54B4B, signal yellow #F6C84C, carbon purple #6B4A7A. Clean airline grotesk headings, condensed tabular numerals, hairline rules, perforation marks, small official stamps only for meaningful state. Restrained soft paper depth, not skeuomorphic clutter. Bright, memorable, orderly, accessible.
Interaction cues: active forecast stub appears validated; expanded itinerary looks unfolded; cancelled or unavailable state would be stamped rather than removed. Use one tear-off motion cue visually, not repeated decoration.
Text (verbatim where visible): "WeatherWise", "Iași, România", "22°", "Mai mult senin", "Ploaie 0%", "Vânt 9 km/h", "UV 4.2", "Deschide itinerarul", "Dimineață", "După-amiază", "Seară", "Grădina Botanică", "Palatul Culturii", "Cinema în seara asta", "Prognoza pe 14 zile".
Constraints: retain realistic weather-app information hierarchy and provider attribution "Weather data by Open-Meteo"; no dark mode; no nostalgic sepia; no torn-paper collage; no excessive rounded cards; no generic dashboard grid; no gradient text; no lorem ipsum; no fake commercial claims; no phone frame; no watermark.
