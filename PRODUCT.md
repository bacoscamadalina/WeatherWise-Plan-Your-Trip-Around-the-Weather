# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

People choosing what to do in a city or village today or during an upcoming trip. They need a useful plan, not only raw weather measurements.

## Product Purpose

WeatherWise searches locations globally, explains a 14-day forecast, and turns each day's conditions into suitable local activities and movie ideas. Success means a user can move from place and date to a practical plan in a few clear steps.

## Positioning

The product combines normalized weather, location, date, and preferences into actionable recommendations. Forecast days are planning entry points rather than passive weather reports.

## Operating Context

The primary workflow is search location, scan the forecast, choose a day, compare weather-matched ideas, and inspect one idea deeply enough to act on it. Iași is the initial laboratory location while the provider architecture and user-facing language remain global-ready.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Rails JSON API with PostgreSQL persistence and provider-neutral geocoding, weather, activity, and movie boundaries.
- Flutter client targeting mobile, desktop, and web.
- The primary search field provides debounced, localized place suggestions after two characters. Results stay visually anchored to the field, stale network responses are discarded, and choosing a suggestion loads that place directly; the full location picker remains available for deliberate searches and ambiguity handling.
- The complete client experience supports persistent English and Romanian language selection, including hourly guidance, advisories, recommendation filters and reasons, itinerary controls, cinema handoff, recommendation details, saved/recent places, ambiguous-place selection, reminder settings, and scheduled reminder copy. Locale changes preserve the active location, selected day, expanded planning context, and loaded forecast; subsequent geocoding and recommendation requests receive the selected language. Provider-authored content is requested from the matching language edition when the source supports it and is never machine-translated by WeatherWise; live availability results and required attribution remain provider-authored.
- Changing language while a day is open automatically reloads its provider-authored recommendations through the newly localized repository. Request generations prevent a slower response in the previous language from overwriting the current results or saved-plan copy.
- Open-Meteo is the default outside tests and powers global search, 14-day weather (including provider-neutral optional daily UV maximum, destination-local sunrise and sunset, daylight duration, and today's current modeled conditions), and on-demand hourly detail. Deterministic mock providers remain available for tests and explicit offline use. OpenStreetMap/Overpass can provide nearby places; Google Places can optionally verify current published activity hours; TMDB, Ticketmaster events, and MovieGlu showtimes are optional and credential-gated.
- Movie curation combines normalized weather and temperature with a stable locality-derived cinematic persona and date-specific rotation. The client keeps each place/date selection eligible when revisited but excludes films already shown from other days and locations during the app session; catalog exhaustion yields fewer ideas rather than hidden repetition.
- Expanding a day loads a destination-timezone hourly ribbon and derives the longest suitable daylight window for outdoor plans. Hourly data is cached in PostgreSQL and remains provider-neutral.
- The forecast overview includes an interactive 14-day temperature-range and rain-chance chart. Selecting a chart day follows the same planning path as selecting its forecast card.
- When available for today, the selected-day hero leads with the current modeled condition and temperature, includes apparent temperature and the destination-local “as of” time, and uses that current condition to choose its atmospheric palette. The daily high/low remains visible alongside optional UV maximum and a destination-local sunrise–sunset range, while provider attribution remains present.
- The selected-day hero exposes the top-ranked real activity, event, or movie as a compact “Best plan” preview before the day is expanded. A real preview is a semantic full-row action that opens its detail screen directly; loading and generic weather fallback states remain non-interactive. It keeps the preview synchronized with search, place changes, refreshes, and day selection; while loading it communicates that local ideas are being matched, and provider failures fall back to the generic weather-planning signal without blocking the forecast.
- Current-condition data stays provider-neutral: `observed_at`, normalized condition, temperature, apparent temperature, precipitation, wind, and daylight state. WMO code 1 is “Mainly clear,” code 2 is “Partly cloudy,” and code 3 is “Overcast,” rather than collapsing all three into one label.
- Forecast responses expose `meta.forecast_updated_at`, and the selected-day hero turns it into a live relative “Updated …” refresh control that advances each minute. The current-condition caption says “Current estimate” with local time, switching to “Last estimate” after the fetch becomes more than 90 minutes old. The refresh control stays inside the responsive fact wrap, uses an opaque high-contrast surface, and demo mode says “Try live weather” instead of claiming live freshness.
- Every Flutter forecast read includes a unique `_request` query value to bypass browser HTTP caching while leaving the Rails forecast TTL authoritative. Manual `refresh=true` requests additionally bypass that server cache, retain the previous forecast with clear feedback on failure, and never downgrade an existing live view to demo data.
- The client deterministically derives one best forecast match for outdoor plans from activity context, rain, wind, temperature comfort, UV exposure, and planning advisories, with UV above 7 reducing the score and the earliest date winning a tie. It explains the match without presenting it as certainty; when every option is poor, the reason includes the selected day's explicit rain and wind values, and a selected day with UV 8 or above adds midday-shade advice without displacing those concrete conditions.
- Deterministic planning advisories highlight conditions that may materially change a day plan, including very high or extreme UV. They are forecast guidance, not official emergency or medical warnings, and must always say so.
- Recommendation ranking gradually penalizes known exposed outdoor ideas when daily UV exceeds 7. At UV 8 or above, the API may attach provider-neutral planning notes with a stable code, severity, and message; indoor ideas and ideas whose setting is unknown are not penalized or annotated. The client places each note directly beneath the primary recommendation reason with semantic labeling and responsive wrapping. The guidance favors shade around midday and sun protection and is neither an official warning nor medical advice.
- Android, iOS, and macOS users can opt into device-local reminders for one followed place, select a local reminder time, and choose all advisories or high-priority conditions only. Permission is requested only after an explicit save; these reminders are not official alerts.
- Seeded recommendations keep Iași usable offline and when live providers fail, including a deterministic high-UV scenario for recommendation guidance.
- Location-symbolic photography is part of the planning experience, not optional decoration. Location payloads accept a licensed `hero_image_url` and `image_attribution`; provider-backed recommendation images flow into both the day-plan strip and recommendation gallery. Iași ships with clearly labeled illustrative assets for offline use. Other places must use imagery attributed to that location or a neutral loading/fallback surface—never another city's landmark.
- Device-local day plans arrange one activity, event, movie, or showtime into each morning, afternoon, and evening slot without sending planning choices to the backend. A deterministic weather-aware action can suggest or complete the three periods from the currently ranked ideas, preserves every manual choice, and never repeats one recommendation across periods.
- Device-local plan writes are serialized from immutable snapshots so rapid edits cannot finish out of order and restore an older plan. A failed write does not block a newer edit from being saved.
- When a saved plan item reappears in the current localized recommendation catalog, its provider title, subtitle, coordinates, indoor status, and recommended timing are refreshed while the user's chosen period remains unchanged.
- A completed or partial day plan can open as a prefilled all-day Google Calendar event. The handoff includes the destination, its timezone, the selected forecast date, the weather summary, and every planned period; WeatherWise does not create or modify calendar events itself.
- When at least one planned activity has provider coordinates, the itinerary can open a walking Google Maps route. Located stops are ordered morning, afternoon, then evening; movies and any idea without real coordinates are omitted rather than assigned a guessed location.
- Each morning, afternoon, and evening row includes the nearest available destination-local hourly forecast: time, normalized condition, temperature, and rain chance. An event or showtime uses its listed start hour; unscheduled ideas use stable 09:00, 15:00, and 20:00 planning anchors.
- The client flags explicitly outdoor itinerary stops when their period is classified as rain, reaches at least 50% rain chance or 35 km/h wind, or has snow or storm conditions. An opt-in adaptation action first moves unscheduled outdoor activities into a free, weather-safe morning or afternoon period allowed by the recommendation's provider-derived timing; only unresolved conflicts request indoor alternatives independently of the visible recommendation filters. Every unaffected choice and the user's active filters are preserved, older saved plans hydrate timing metadata from the current catalog when available, and the complete automatic change can be undone immediately without overwriting later manual edits. Partial adaptations state exactly how many outdoor stops remain weather-exposed instead of implying the whole plan is resolved.
- Up to 12 saved places and the five most recent locations are stored on the device for quick return visits; they are not an account or personalized cloud history.
- Up to 12 optional named preference profiles save recommendation filters locally on the device.
- Multi-day trips persist in PostgreSQL as a trip, forecast-linked days, and ordered itinerary items. The planner accepts a one-to-fourteen-day forecast window, arrival and departure times, pace, transport mode, a maximum return radius of 50 km, optional culture/nature/entertainment interests, dietary intent, and meal budget, then composes breakfast, morning, lunch, afternoon, dinner, and evening slots without repeating selected recommendations. The first and last dates become explicit partial days when the travel times shorten the pace-defined schedule; only complete periods inside that availability window are admitted, and the shortened window remains visible in both generated and saved plans. One provider range request supplies destination-local hourly evidence for the full trip: each slot stores its worst relevant condition, temperature range, rain probability, wind, and daylight status; indoor ideas are preferred during wet intervals, while outdoor ideas are admitted only in a safe window. Every selected discovery explains the concrete interval evidence behind that choice, while each day summarizes the overall indoor/outdoor weather strategy; the original ranking reason remains available as metadata. Daily weather remains an explicitly labeled fallback if hourly data is unavailable. Pace changes the actual schedule rather than only its label: relaxed days begin at 09:00, plan two main discoveries, keep longer pauses, and leave the evening open; balanced days begin at 08:30 with three discoveries and comfortable buffers; active days begin at 07:30 with longer exploration windows and a full evening. Known provider durations must fit the selected time window and event start times remain authoritative. After weather compatibility and broad quality bands, proximity to the previous located stop breaks close ranking ties so a day does not zig-zag merely for a marginal score difference. A provider-neutral routing pass follows every located stop chronologically and can attach provider-calculated point-to-point duration, distance, origin, departure, arrival, transport mode, schedule-fit status, and the final return to the base location. The return leg records its departure and estimated arrival, compares that arrival with the pace-defined day end or final-day departure boundary, and exposes the overrun when the traveler would return late. The day payload also derives one logistics summary: planned and located stops, total travel time, total distance only when all legs are known, calculation state, and return time. Google Routes is the first optional adapter. Transit asks to arrive by the first stop's time and departs subsequent legs when the previous stop ends. Flexible activities move later when a route exceeds the gap but still fits the day; fixed or impossible conflicts remain explicit. Without a calculated route, the transport-dependent rhythm buffer and arrival are labeled as estimates rather than fabricated precision. Interests constrain activity discovery while weather eligibility remains authoritative.
- Trip dining uses a provider-neutral boundary. Google Places is the first optional adapter and applies supported price-level filters plus dietary text-search intent before admitting only operational venues rated at least 4.0 with at least 10 reviews; without live credentials, meal slots stay visibly unfilled instead of using invented venues or ratings. Dietary results are discovery matches, not allergen or cross-contamination guarantees, so the client requires direct verification with the venue.
- Email/password accounts use bcrypt password hashes and expiring bearer sessions whose raw tokens are never stored by Rails. Flutter keeps the session token in platform secure storage, restores the user on launch, and exposes sign-up, sign-in, sign-out, and a personal trip library. The core weather and anonymous planning path remains available without an account.
- Anonymous planning returns the complete generated itinerary only in the create response. Reading, replanning, or replacing a stop later requires the authenticated owner; sequential database IDs never grant access to a guest or another account.
- Trips created while authenticated belong to that user, appear only in their personal library, and can be opened or deleted there. A traveler may edit the trip dates, party size, daily pace, transport, maximum return radius, interests, dietary intent, and budget, then rebuild the itinerary from a freshly requested forecast. The new settings and replacement itinerary commit as one complete result; the previous settings and plan remain intact if validation, weather, or recommendation providers fail. Another account receives no existence signal for a private trip. Cross-device preference synchronization, password reset/email verification, guaranteed real-world opening status, in-app booking, and personalized history remain future work. Live provider data is not a guarantee against unexpected closures.
- A traveler may replace one saved itinerary stop without rebuilding the full trip. Activities, events, and movies are reselected against that day's forecast, time period, and trip radius; meal replacements still require a real provider venue above the dining quality threshold. Any recommendation or venue already used elsewhere in the trip is excluded. The requested item keeps its time and position, every other stop stays unchanged, and that day's point-to-point route is recalculated. Provider failure or catalog exhaustion preserves the current stop with an explicit retry path.
- Provider attribution must remain visible wherever provider data is presented.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is WeatherWise. Its voice is calm, practical, encouraging, and honest about uncertainty or unverified availability.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains working Rails and Flutter applications, a seeded Iași catalog, live OpenStreetMap discovery, weather-aware ranking, provider attribution assets, and automated tests. No commercial claims, user testimonials, proprietary venue data, or booking relationships exist and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make every forecast actionable.
- Prefer clear reasons over opaque ranking.
- Keep provider integrations interchangeable and failures graceful.
- Distinguish provider data from verified real-world availability.
- Distinguish planning advisories from official local weather warnings.
- Keep the core planning path useful without an account.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use platform-native navigation and controls, scalable semantic typography, sufficient contrast, non-color-only status cues, and touch targets suitable for iOS and Android.
